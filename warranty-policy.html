Warranty Policy


Free 60-Day Warranty

All products get a free 60-Day Warranty. This covers manufacturing defects or failures that happen after the first few weeks of using the product that can be replaced at no additional cost.

One-Year Limited Warranty

OptiCore provides a one-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects, hardware defects, and unexpected hardware failures. This warranty covers all of the products shown on the product page. This warranty is valid for one year from the date of receiving the product. This warranty has a flat rate of $49.99 for all products.

If a defect occurs during the warranty period, customers may request an inspection to see what is wrong with the product, following the terms of the warranty policy. For approved claims, OptiCore will cover the replacement costs including shipping and send out a replacement.

What is not covered?

The warranty does not cover any damages that involve accidents, liquid spills, improper handling, misuse, unauthorized modifications, bent pins (such as CPU sockets), and normal cosmic wear and tear.

Extended Two-Year Warranty with Accidental Damage

This two-year warranty provides more coverage than our standard one-year warranty. This covers everything from the one-year warranty along with protection from accidental damages. This warranty has a flat rate of $124.99.

What's Covered

  • Everything from the standard one-year warranty
  • Damage from drops and falls
  • Liquid Spills/Damage
  • Bent Pins

What's Not Covered

  • Intentional Damages
  • Theft
  • Loss
  • Unauthorized Modifications
  • Misuse
  • Damage caused by operating above the hardware's limits
  • Cosmic wear and tear

Warranty for Laptops

When paying one or two year warranties for a laptop, it also covers repairs and replacements including batteries, displays, and ports from defects.

Customer Support

Submit proof of purchase and identify the problem so that our support team can check for the cause of the issue and decide whether it needs to be replaced or repaired. If a product (i.e. laptops) needs to be repaired, customers can ship their product back to us and our certified technicians will repair them. If a product cannot be repaired, customers will be given a replacement while shipping the defective product back. The cost will depend on the product type and if they have an active warranty.

Typical price for laptop repairs/replacements without warranty:

  • Laptop Battery Replacement: $180
  • Laptop Display: $200
  • Laptop Ports: $125
  • Other Laptop Repairs: Up to $250

Typical price for laptop repairs/replacements with warranty:

  • Laptop Battery Replacement: $0
  • Laptop Display: $0
  • Laptop Ports: $0
  • Other Laptop Repairs: Up to $99
