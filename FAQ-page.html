Our products are for people who are looking for computer parts to upgrade or build their computer whether they are a gamer, a student, a professional, or for general computer use.

Product & Customer Questions


Where are your products manufactured?

Just like most tech hardware companies, we don't manufacture them in the U.S., we instead manufacture parts internationally, most specifically Asia due to the global supply chains of tech parts including Taiwan, as they are the world's most dominant semiconductor manufacturer for CPUs and GPUs, and South Korea for RAM.

Are your computer parts compatible with my current computer?

Our desktop CPU and GPU are only compatible with our own motherboard and can't be used on any other motherboards. For RAM and SSD, they are compatible with specific AMD and Intel motherboards including AMD's B650, X670, and B850; and Intel's LGA 1700 and LGA 1851 series.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we offer standard international shipping options. Shipping rates and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location.

Do all of your products come with a warranty?

Yes. All of our products come with a free 60-day warranty. You can upgrade the warranty to the One-Year Warranty or the Extended Two-Year Warranty with Accidental Damage. If the computer part fails or breaks due to factory defects, you can return it at no additional cost to you and we will ship a replacement or repair it. Visit our Warranty Policy for more information.
